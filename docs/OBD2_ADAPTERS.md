# OBD2 Bluetooth Adapter Guide

What to buy, what to avoid, and where the OBD2 port is on common cars.

---

## What to Look For

- Bluetooth (not WiFi)
- ELM327 chipset or compatible
- Bluetooth 4.0+ (BLE) preferred
- Low power draw so it won't kill your car battery
- Compatible with Torque Pro

---

## What to Avoid

- WiFi-only adapters: they drain your phone battery and are unreliable
- Very cheap clones under Rs. 500: often fail to connect or drop data
- ELM327 with version below 1.5: may not support all protocols

---

## OBD2 Port Locations

### Honda Elevate (2025)

Under the dashboard on the driver's side, near the steering column. Look for a 16-pin trapezoidal connector. You might need to remove a small cover panel.

For more Honda Elevate-specific setup tips, see the [Honda Elevate Guide](HONDA_ELEVATE.md).

### Most Cars

The OBD2 port is usually under the dashboard on the driver's side, within reach of the steering wheel. It's a 16-pin trapezoidal connector. Some cars hide it behind a small panel or near the fuse box.

---

## How to Pair

1. Plug the OBD2 adapter into your car's diagnostic port
2. Turn the ignition to ON (engine can be off)
3. On your phone, go to Settings > Bluetooth
4. Find the adapter (usually named `OBDII` or `Vlink`) and pair with it
5. Default PIN is usually `1234` or `0000`
6. Don't open Torque Pro yet, just complete the Bluetooth pairing
